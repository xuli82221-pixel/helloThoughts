## English

Thoughts is free and open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Donations are optional: they unlock no features, create no subscription and do not purchase support or a release commitment. You can also help by reporting issues, improving translations or contributing documentation; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The maintainer's existing public donation configuration is [release/sponsorship.json](release/sponsorship.json):

- Network: **Base mainnet** (chain ID `8453`).
- Address: **`0x2579590c8528A34278b5532931711cE2eEAF2406`**.
- No asset or amount is prescribed. The QR contains **only the address** and does not select the network for you.

![Address-only donation QR; select Base mainnet separately](web/public/assets/support-base-address-qr.png)

If you choose to donate, use your own wallet and verify the full address and Base network there. Thoughts does not connect wallets, request private keys/recovery phrases, submit transactions, process payments or automatically collect donation details. Wallet providers and public blockchain records have their own privacy implications; see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). This page does not establish a charity, tax deduction or refund promise.
